Bibliography
Gates Millennium Scholars (GMS) Survey Data Cohort 5, United States, 2004-
2009

This bibliography includes published and unpublished works that are related 
to ICPSR study 34439. It includes works that are based on primary
or secondary analysis of the data, or which describe or critique those
data or the collection methodology. 

If you publish a work or know of other works that are based on these data,
please send the complete citation and name of the ICPSR study used to:
icpsr-biblio-notify@umich.edu 


1. DesJardins, Stephen L.; McCall, Brian P.; Ott, Molly; Kim, Jiyun, "A
quasi-experimental investigation of how the Gates Millennium Scholars
Program is related to college students' time use and activities."
Educational Evaluation and Policy Analysis. 2010, 32, (4), 456 - 475. DOI:
10.3102/0162373710380739

2. Lee, Lisa; Zimowski, Michele; and Schacht, Stephen, "The Fifth Cohort of
Gates Millennium Scholars One Year After High School: A Comparative
Analysis of the Background Characteristics, Early Outcomes and Experiences
of the Entering Freshman Class of 2004." Chicago, IL: National Opinion
Research Center (NORC), Apr 2007.

3. National Opinion Research Center (NORC), "GMS Tracking and Longitudinal
Study Newsletter." Chicago, IL: National Opinion Research Center (NORC),
2005.

4. Oseguera, Leticia; Hurtado, Sylvia; St. John, Edward P.; Santos, Jose
Luis, "Access of Low-income Students of Color to College." Presented at the
American Council of Education (ACE), 2005.

5. Peteet, B.; Bates, L.; McCuistian, C.; Lige, Q., "Personal attitudes and
STEM major selection in academically capable ethnic minority students."
Presented at the annual meeting of the Association of Black Psychologists,
Las Vegas, NV, Jul 2015.

6. Trent, William T., "Dropouts: A Focused Analysis." 2006..

Effects of Support and Community." Dissertation, Baylor University, 2017.
